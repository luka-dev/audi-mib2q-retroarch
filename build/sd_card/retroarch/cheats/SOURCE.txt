Only cheat collections for the QNX image's supported platforms are installed
under cheats/: Game Boy Advance, Nintendo 64, PlayStation, and PlayStation
Portable. Git checkout applies the upstream `*.cht text eol=lf` rule; cheat
contents are otherwise unchanged. The launcher seeds the writable collection
to /fs/sda0/retroarch/cheats. COPYING is the upstream CC BY-SA 4.0 license file.
