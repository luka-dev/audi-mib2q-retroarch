QNX HID output-report rumble profiles

The generic driver format is local to this port.  The shipped PS4/PS5 report
IDs, field offsets, flags, Bluetooth sequence byte and CRC32 seed follow the
Linux hid-playstation implementation:
  https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/drivers/hid/hid-playstation.c

The DualShock 3, 8BitDo and Xbox Bluetooth report templates follow SDL's
zlib-licensed HIDAPI drivers at commit
616b8fb338c55249a4a9d6c2245f0a10410f9aec:
  https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/main/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_ps3.c
  https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/main/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_8bitdo.c
  https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/main/src/joystick/hidapi/SDL_hidapi_xboxone.c

Cross-checked: 2026-08-07

Profiles are keyed by lower-case hexadecimal VID_PID_REPORTID.  Unknown output
HID layouts are never guessed: no matching profile means rumble is left
disabled. XUSB/GIP devices use their fixed vendor-protocol packets in the QNX
driver instead of these HID profiles. Additional HID controllers can be
supported without rebuilding RetroArch by adding a validated profile here.
